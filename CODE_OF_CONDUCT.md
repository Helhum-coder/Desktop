# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, and to provide a safe, welcoming environment free from abuse,
threats, and misconduct.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards others
- Respecting intellectual property rights
- Supporting a safe environment for all

### Unacceptable Behaviors

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation of any kind
- Abusive, derogatory, or discriminatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Intellectual property theft or unauthorized use of work
- Criminal activity or encouragement thereof
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe environment
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly.

### Consequences

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code
of Conduct. They may also ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for
behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual
is representing the project in public spaces.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, with additional provisions for safety and intellectual property protection.

## Safety First

**Your safety and well-being matter.** If you have experienced or witnessed
abuse, harassment, or feel unsafe in any way:

1. Your concerns will be taken seriously
2. You have the right to a safe environment
3. Appropriate action will be taken
4. Confidentiality will be maintained

No one should have to endure abuse or feel unsafe while contributing to or
using this project.

## Intellectual Property Protection

All intellectual property rights are respected and protected. Contributors
retain their rights to their work, and all contributions are properly
acknowledged and attributed.

---

**Zero Tolerance**: We have zero tolerance for abuse, harassment, threats,
or any behavior that compromises safety or violates intellectual property rights.
